# Identify Outcome

Use this skill when the intended outcome is not clear. Frontload this work so that the person doesn't need to intervene in the work at a later time.

Interview the user until you have a shared understanding of the outcome, rooted in specific measurable items.

Ask questions in many rounds. Aim to ask 3 questions per round and at most 5 rounds. Provide multiple choices when possible, but always allow the user to enter in free form text. Use the answers from the previous rounds to shape the later questions.

Finding the right questions to ask is your job. Use information from your context and environment to guide the questions. Don't ask the user anything that you could look up yourself.

Understand how the outcome will be measured, validated, and determined to be acceptable by the user.

The session is done when the outcome is clear, after 5 rounds, or when the user asks to stop.
