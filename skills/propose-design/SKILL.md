# Propose Design

The goal of this stage is to explain to the user how something will be solved. A good design will have:

- Explanation of the core goal
- Constraints or requirements
- Background or related material that is highly relevant to the design
- High level overview of the design
- Section with details about design, diving into a level of detail when it's necessary to explain a complicated or controversial part of the design.
- Sections on security, privacy, and production readiness
- How the design will be validated

Communicate to the user as if they were new to the codebase or you were sharing the design with members from other teams. Don't use overly complex jargon, AI slop words, or overly terse or ambiguous sentences.

If the design is trivial, has few decision points, only has one clear choice, or you are very confident in the design, share the design with the user but continue to the next step.
